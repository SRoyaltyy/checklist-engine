**Ruling proposals part 3: indicator groups — Bollinger / Donchian / ATR / ADX / Ichimoku**

All 14 families below use standard indicator constants (Bollinger20,2; Donchian20; ATR14-Wilder; ADX14/DI-Wilder; Ichimoku 9/26/52). Documented constants are in parentheses; each needs only "approved" (amend any constant you want changed). Squeeze/Keltner is at the end — it alone needs a constants ruling.

---

**Bollinger20,2** (basis: SMA20 ± 2×population-σ20 of closes; %B = (C−lower)/(upper−lower); close-basis throughout):
9. `reentry` (21 × {from_below,from_above,none}): an excursion = a close beyond a band; `from_below` = some window bar closed < lower band and a LATER bar closes back inside [lower, upper]; mirror `from_above`; the most recent excursion decides; no excursion → none.
10. `band_walk` (21 × {upper,lower,none}, dial C109 = run length): exists a run of ≥ C109 consecutive closes ≥ upper band → upper (mirror lower); else none.
11. `pctb_path` (17 × {rising,falling,flat}): %B last minus first over the window; flat when |Δ%B| ≤ 0.05 (no dial registered — documented constant).
12. `time_upper` (17 × {upper,lower,balanced}, dial C110 = the 0.5 %B line): share of window bars with %B > 0.5; upper when share ≥ 2/3, lower when ≤ 1/3, else balanced (2/3, 1/3 documented constants).

**Donchian20** (channel = highest high / lowest low of the PRIOR 20 bars, current bar excluded):
13. `fail_shape` (17 × {bull,bear}×{same_day,multi_day,none}, dial C118 = 2): latest failed break decides — **bear** = failed break ABOVE (high > prior-20 high), **bull** = failed break BELOW (mirror); same_day = the breaking bar's close back inside the channel; multi_day = ≥ C118 consecutive closes beyond the channel followed by a close back inside; none = no failed break.

**ATR14 (Wilder):**
14. `range_max_position` (17 × {start,middle,end}): position tertile of the window's widest-range bar (ties → first occurrence).
15. `vol_direction_link` (17 × {down_vol,up_vol,neutral}): sum of ranges of down bars vs up bars in the window; down_vol when down-sum > 1.25×up-sum (mirror 0.8), else neutral (documented 1.25/0.8 band).

**ADX14 / +DI / −DI (all Wilder 14):**
16. `adx_slope` (17 × {rising,falling,flat}): ADX last minus first over the window; flat when |ΔADX| ≤ 1.0 point (documented constant).
17. `di_cross` (17 × {bull,bear,none}): latest +DI/−DI cross in the window decides; bull = +DI crossed above −DI most recently (and +DI > −DI at D−1); mirror bear; none = no cross.
18. `peak_turn` (17 × {yes,no}): a local ADX maximum (greater than the bars either side, first occurrence scanning back from D−1) followed by ≥ 2 consecutive declining ADX bars ending at D−1.
19. `time_trending` (17 × {most,some,none}, dial C238 = 25): share of window bars with ADX > C238; most ≥ 2/3, some ≥ 1/3, else none (documented 2/3, 1/3).
20. `trend_strength_route` (17 × 9 pairs, dials C236 = 25 / C237 = 15): zone of the FIRST and LAST window bar's ADX — trending (ADX > C236) / weak (ADX < C237) / mid (between) — reported as a start→end pair.

**Ichimoku (tenkan 9 / kijun 26 / senkou A,B 52 / chikou 26-back; warmup 52):**
21. `tk_cross` (14 × {bull,bear,none}): latest tenkan/kijun cross in window decides (bull = tenkan above kijun at D−1 after the latest cross).
22. `cloud_twist` (14 × {bull,bear,none}): latest senkou A/B cross within the cloud span visible at D−1; bull = A above B after it.
23. `cloud_route` (14 × 9 pairs): close vs cloud (above top / inside / below bottom) at the window's first and last bar, as a start→end pair.
24. `time_above` (14 × {most,some,none}): share of window bars with close > cloud top; most ≥ 2/3, some ≥ 1/3, else none (documented).
25. `kijun_tests` (14 × {support_held,resistance_held,broke,none}): a test = a window bar whose range straddles the kijun; from above = support test — held if the next 3 closes stay ≥ kijun, else broke; mirror from below = resistance test; the most recent test decides.
26. `chikou_route` (14 × {improving,worsening,same}): chikou vs the price 26 bars back — improving = below→above across the window (mirror worsening), same = no side change.
