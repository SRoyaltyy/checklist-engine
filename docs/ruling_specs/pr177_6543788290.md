**#43 `down_vs_up`** (return_path × daily_returns, 21 qids, ~63 defs) — "down moves bigger than up moves?" {downs_bigger, ups_bigger, equal}
- Measure: **mean |return|** of the window's down moves (r<0) vs up moves (r>0); zero returns excluded from both sides.
- downs_bigger = mean_down > mean_up; ups_bigger = mean_up > mean_down; equal = exact equality.
- Documented rules: a side with **zero moves cannot be bigger** (other side wins if it has any move); a window with moves on neither side (all-zero returns) is 0/0 → NOT_EVALUABLE.
- **Amendable point:** totals (Σ|down| vs Σ|up|) instead of means — the two readings diverge when the up/down counts differ, so picking one is the ruling. I propose means ("moves bigger" = per-move size).

**#44 `price_inside`** (moving_averages × MA_group(10/20/50/200), 21 qids, ~42 defs) — "share of bars with close between fastest and slowest MA?" {mostly_inside, mostly_outside}
- Window closes between **SMA10 and SMA200** (the group's documented fastest/slowest pair, stack_flip convention); share = count(min(MA10,MA200) < C < max(MA10,MA200))/lb (strict; a close exactly on an MA is outside).
- **mostly_inside = share ≥ 0.6, mostly_outside = share ≤ 0.4** — exact above_share precedent; the (0.4, 0.6) dead band answers NOT_EVALUABLE on both outcomes (excluded, never NO), since only two outcomes exist.
- **Amendable point:** single-threshold ≥ 0.5 split instead of the 0.6/0.4 + dead band.

**#45 `fan_out`** (MA_group, 21 qids, ~63 defs) — "spread change last third vs first?" {fanning, converging, steady}
- Signed spread s(t) = SMA10(t) − SMA200(t) over the window; s_first = mean of the **first third** of window bars, s_last = mean of the **last third** (per the question text; thirds by bar count, remainder bars to the middle).
- **fanning = s_last > 1.25 × s_first; converging = s_last < 0.8 × s_first; steady otherwise** — the same 1.25/0.8 band constants proposed in part 3 (vol-direction), kept consistent.
- s_first = 0 → ratio undefined → NOT_EVALUABLE (0/0 precedent). Negative s_first (bear stack): the same inequalities apply to the signed spread, so fanning in a bear stack = more negative — document, amendable to |spread| if James prefers amplitude.
- **Amendable point:** signed vs absolute spread.

**#46 `stack_route`** (MA_group, 21 qids, ~84 defs) — "strict stack state start->end?" {full_bull, partial_bull, partial_bear, full_bear(reversed)}
- Reading proposed: the four outcomes are the **end-of-window stack state** ("start->end" describes the window over which the stack is assessed): at the last window bar, with all four SMAs strictly ordered — **full_bull** = 10>20>50>200; **full_bear** = 200>50>20>10 (strict reverse); **partial_bull** = not full, but SMA10 > SMA200; **partial_bear** = everything else (SMA10 ≤ SMA200 and not full_bear).
- Any exact equality among the four SMAs at the measurement bar → NOT_EVALUABLE (weekly_stack degenerate precedent).
- **Amendable point:** if "(reversed) pairs" instead means start→end *transition* outcomes, say so — but with 4 outcomes and 9 possible (start,end) pairs that reading can't fill the outcome set, which is why I propose the end-state reading.

**#47 `time_bull_stack`** (MA_group, 21 qids, ~63 defs) — "share of bars bull-stacked?" {most, some, none}
- bull-stacked bar = strict 10>20>50>200 at that bar (any equality → that bar not bull-stacked; weekly_stack strictness). share = bull-stacked bars / lb over the window.
- **most = share ≥ 2/3; none = share = 0; some = everything in between** — the 2/3 majority constant from part 2, kept consistent.
